Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Movie Hub. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy will inform you as to how we look after your data when you visit our website.

2. Information We Collect

Movie Hub is designed to be a seamless experience. We currently do not require user accounts, meaning we do not collect personal identifying information like names, addresses, or credit card numbers. We may collect anonymous usage data to improve our search functionality.

3. Third-Party Services

Our website utilizes the OMDB API to fetch movie data, posters, and ratings. When you search for a movie, your search query is processed through their servers. Please refer to their respective privacy policies for more information on how they handle data.

4. Cookies

We may use essential cookies to ensure our website functions properly and to save your theme preferences. You can set your browser to refuse all or some browser cookies, but this may affect how the website functions.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or our privacy practices, please contact us via our Contact Page.
